About the RHL Project

The RHL Project works to reduce wealth risks among vulnerable coastal communities and strengthen their capacity to adapt to climate-related challenges.

Through resilient housing, diversified livelihoods, climate-adaptive agriculture, crab-related livelihoods and tree plantation, the project supports practical solutions at household and community level.

Our Key Interventions

Five practical approaches supporting resilient coastal livelihoods and environmental adaptation.

🏠

Climate Resilient Homestead

Supporting households with climate-adaptive homestead solutions.

🐐

Slatted Goat House

Improved goat-rearing facilities for safer and more resilient livelihoods.

🦀

Crab Fattening / Nursery

Livelihood diversification through crab-related production activities.

🥬

Soil-Tolerant Vegetables

Homestead cultivation adapted to challenging coastal soil conditions.

🌳

Tree Plantation

Strengthening household and community-level environmental resilience.
